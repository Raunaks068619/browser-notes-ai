# Privacy Policy for Lo-Note

**Last Updated: February 1, 2026**

## Overview

Lo-Note ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how Lo-Note handles your data.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any personal data.** Lo-Note is designed with privacy as a core principle.

### What Data is Stored Locally

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's IndexedDB:

- **Notes Content** - Your notes and documents
- **Editor Settings** - Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
- **AI API Keys** - If you choose to use AI features (stored encrypted in IndexedDB)

### What Data is NOT Collected

- We do NOT collect any analytics
- We do NOT track your usage
- We do NOT transmit your notes to our servers (we don't have any servers)
- We do NOT share any data with third parties

## AI Features (Optional)

If you choose to use the AI assistant features:

- You must provide your own API key (OpenAI or Google Gemini)
- Your API key is stored locally in your browser only
- When you use AI features, your notes are sent to the AI provider you configured (OpenAI or Google)
- We do NOT see, store, or have access to your API keys or AI requests
- Please review the privacy policies of your chosen AI provider:
  - [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
  - [Google AI Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Permissions Explanation

Lo-Note requests the following Chrome permissions:

- **sidePanel** - To display the note-taking interface in Chrome's side panel
- **storage** - To save your notes locally in IndexedDB

We do NOT request:
- Network access
- Access to your browsing history
- Access to other websites' data

## Data Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- No cloud synchronization (your data never leaves your browser)
- API keys are stored only in your browser's secure storage

## Children's Privacy

Lo-Note does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The extension is designed for general use and does not target children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- You can delete all data by removing the extension
- You can view your data in Chrome DevTools (Application → IndexedDB)
- You have complete control over your data
